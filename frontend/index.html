Scientific notes

Assumptions

  • Concentrations are expressed in mg/L of the listed ionic species.
  • SO4 input represents sulfate, not elemental sulfur.
  • The optimization only adds nutrients; it does not model dilution or nutrient removal.
  • Fertilizer ions are assumed to fully dissociate.
  • Stock concentrations are grams of fertilizer per liter of stock solution.
  • Actual fertilizer purity and hydration state must be verified before physical dosing.
Check your chemical form. Molecular weights currently assume anhydrous fertilizer formulas. If hydrated salts are used to prepare stock solutions, enter or configure the molecular weight corresponding to the actual chemical product.
